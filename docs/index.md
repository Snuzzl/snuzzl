# Welcome to Snuzzl's documentation!

**Snuzzl** is an app that allows users to manage their schedule with tasks, track their life through metrics and compete with other users.  
The goal of Snuzzl is to improve the users wellbeing. This done through a [*Wellbeing score*](features/metrics.md#wellbeing-score) that reflects their interaction with the apps features, giving them an indicator for where they can improve their wellbeing.

Look at [install & run](install.md) for instructions on installing and running the app.

Or check out the [features](features/index.md) section for further information.

!!! note

    This project is under active development.
